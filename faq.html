Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, equipment, and care process.

We offer a comprehensive range of services tailored for elderly care, including in-home nursing, medical equipment rental and sales, and care coordination. Our in-home nursing includes medication management, wound care, and daily living assistance. We provide high-quality equipment like mobility aids, respiratory devices, and monitoring tools, available for rent or purchase. Our care coordination service connects families with healthcare providers to create personalized care plans, ensuring seamless support for seniors.

Choosing the right equipment depends on your specific needs and medical requirements. Our team offers free, personalized consultations to assess your situation, whether it’s for mobility, respiratory support, or daily living aids. We consider factors like your home environment, physical condition, and budget to recommend the best options. Contact us via our website or call (123) 456-7890 to schedule a consultation, and we’ll guide you through the process, including equipment trials if needed.

Yes, we provide free delivery and professional setup for all our medical equipment within our service area. Our trained technicians ensure the equipment is installed correctly and provide hands-on training to you and your caregivers on safe usage. We also offer ongoing maintenance for rented equipment and support for purchased items. To arrange delivery, contact us through our website or call (123) 456-7890, and we’ll schedule a convenient time.

Absolutely, all our nurses are fully licensed and certified, with specialized training in geriatric care. They hold credentials such as Registered Nurse (RN) or Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN) certifications and undergo regular training to stay updated on best practices. Our nurses are also background-checked and vetted for compassion and professionalism, ensuring high-quality, trustworthy care for your loved ones.

Yes, we offer flexible rental options for most of our medical equipment, including wheelchairs, hospital beds, oxygen concentrators, and more.
